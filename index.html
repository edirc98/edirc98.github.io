Career

April 2021 — Present

Unity Developer

IDIBAPS · Institut d'Investigacions Biomèdiques August Pi i Sunyer · Barcelona

  • Developed and maintained 10+ Unity applications for interactive simulation and clinical research, delivered as part of EU-funded projects (VR-PerGenere, XR-Pain / EMIL XR).
  • Designed and led the Unity architecture of XR-Pain alongside one collaborator — a real-time interactive application validated in a Randomised Controlled Trial (RCT) with 100 patients.

  • Collaborated closely with researchers, clinicians, and designers in an interdisciplinary team, translating scientific requirements into robust, reusable Unity systems.
